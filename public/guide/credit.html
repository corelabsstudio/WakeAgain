Site Credit Score

Behavior is auto-tracked on a 0–100 scale. The base score is low; real completed deals earn most of the points. The formula isn't hidden. It's a reference score.

Trust level (Lv0–Lv3) = eligibility, e.g. real name · settlement account
Site credit score = reputation, e.g. deal completion · payment behavior (automatic)
Sign-up + Lv2 + Lv3 alone gets you about 40 points (Average/New) — the "Trusted" grade needs an actual deal history.

Buyer badges (Active buyer, etc.)

The badge other users and sellers see rises with your completed purchase count. It's separate from your credit score/Lv (eligibility) — it signals "this person actually buys and follows through."

Disclosure

Placing a bid is treated as agreeing to pay, to meet the stated payment deadline, and to yield to the next bidder if unpaid. (The 1-hour auto-timer and automatic runner-up transfer apply once payments are live. For now this is the operating policy.) Missed payments may affect your site credit score. Score and buyer badges are shown on listings, profiles, and bid lists.
