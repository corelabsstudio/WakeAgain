Account Deletion Request

App / service name: WakeAgain
Developer / operator: CoreLabs
This page is the account-deletion notice shown in the Google Play Store listing.

Want to delete your account?

You can request deletion of your WakeAgain account and related data. Please follow the steps below. Once confirmed, we process the request within a reasonable period (target: within 14 business days).

1. How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to the address below from the email you signed up with.
    corelabs.studio@gmail.com
  2. Example email subject:
    [WakeAgain] Account Deletion Request
  3. Please include the following in the email body.
    • App name: WakeAgain
    • Developer: CoreLabs
    • Account email to delete (same as your sign-up email)
    • (Optional) Display name on the account · approximate sign-up date
    • A line stating "I am requesting deletion of my account and related data"
  4. For identity verification, we may ask you to confirm by replying from your sign-up email. We proceed with deletion once confirmed.
  5. Once processing is complete, we'll send a result notice to the email you requested from.

Compose deletion request email

2. Data that gets deleted

Once your request is confirmed, the following is deleted in principle.

CategoryDetails
Account identityEmail, display name, password hash, birth date, and other sign-up info
Identity (Lv2)Real name, phone, and other identity info saved on your profile
Settlement (Lv3)Account holder name, bank, account number, and other settlement info
Login · sessionsApp/web login sessions and tokens (invalidated immediately)
Notifications · settingsNotification and app settings tied to your account
Coupons heldUnused coupons registered to that account

Service data tied to your account — public listings, interest registrations, bids, etc. — is deleted, de-identified, or separated depending on deal/dispute status. (If a deal is in progress, deletion may follow after it's completed or resolved.)

3. Data that may be retained · retention period

Data required by law, or needed to prevent disputes or fraudulent use, may be retained for a period after your deletion request before being destroyed. In that case it is not used for any other purpose and is stored separately.

CategoryRetention period (per applicable law/policy)
Contract, transaction, payment, and fee records In principle, 5 years (per Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, etc.)
Payment and goods-supply records In principle, 5 years
Consumer complaint / dispute-handling records In principle, 3 years
Access logs, etc. Per applicable law (e.g. Protection of Communications Secrets Act, where relevant)
Fraud/abuse and sanction history (minimal identifiers) Scoped to preventing signup abuse and fraud; reviewed and destroyed within up to 1 year if needed

Data is destroyed without delay once its legal retention period ends. For details on processing purposes, see the Privacy Policy.

4. Processing time · outcome

  • We target processing within 14 business days after your request is received and identity is confirmed.
  • If a large amount of data is subject to legal retention, some data may remain for the periods above instead of being deleted immediately.
  • We'll notify you by email whether deletion is complete or some data is being retained.
