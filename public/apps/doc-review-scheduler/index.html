Doc Review Scheduler — Documentation

Confluence Cloud app by CoreLabs · Last updated 25 August 2026

What it does

Attach a review date to a Confluence page. When the date passes, the page owner gets one comment mentioning them, and a button on the page to clear it. The app runs on Atlassian Forge inside your own Cloud tenancy.

Install

  1. Open the app on the Atlassian Marketplace and select Try it free or Buy now.
  2. A Confluence site admin approves the installation.
  3. No configuration is needed. The app appears on every page byline immediately.

Set a review schedule

  1. Open any Confluence page.
  2. Select Review status in the byline, next to the page title.
  3. Choose a cycle — every 3, 6 or 12 months — and select Set review schedule.

The next review date is calculated from today. Month ends are clamped: 31 January plus one month is 28 February.

Mark a page reviewed

Select Mark reviewed in the panel. The next review date moves forward by the cycle length, counted from the day you pressed it — not from the old due date. Use Remove to drop the schedule from the page entirely.

The weekly digest

Once a week the app looks for pages whose review date has passed, groups them by page owner, and posts a single comment on Confluence mentioning that owner.

Automation quotaThe app runs on its own schedule and spends none of your Confluence automation quota.

Status badges

On trackThe review date is today or later.
OverdueThe review date has passed. The page is included in the next weekly digest.

Licensing

Sites with 10 users or fewer use the app at no cost. Above that, the app is billed per user through Atlassian. Without an active licence you can still see existing review dates, but setting and clearing them is disabled — no data is deleted.

What the app does not do yet

  • No space-level dashboard or cross-space report — the app works page by page.
  • English only.
  • Confluence Cloud only. Data Center and Server are not supported.
  • No bulk assignment of review dates across many pages.

Data and privacy

The app stores a review interval and two dates per page, in Forge storage inside your own tenancy. It stores no account identifiers, names or email addresses, uses no third-party services, and makes no network calls outside Atlassian. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
