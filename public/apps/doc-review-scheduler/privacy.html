Privacy Policy — Doc Review Scheduler

Confluence Cloud app by CoreLabs · Last updated 25 August 2026

1. Summary

Doc Review Scheduler stores the minimum needed to remind the right person that a page is due for review. It runs entirely on Atlassian infrastructure (Atlassian Forge). We operate no servers of our own and no data leaves Atlassia’ platform.

2. What the app stores

DataPage ID, review interval, next review date, last reviewed date
Personal dataNone. The app stores no user identifiers, names, email addresses or IP addresses
WhereAtlassian Forge storage, inside your own Atlassian Cloud tenancy
Not storedPage content, analytics or tracking data

The app deliberately does not store Atlassian accountId values. When the weekly digest runs it looks up the current page owner from Confluence at that moment, mentions them in a comment, and discards the identifier. Nothing about a person is written to storage.

3. What the app does with it

Once a week a scheduled job looks for pages whose review date has passed and posts a single comment on Confluence mentioning the assigned reviewer. Notification delivery is handled by Confluence itself and follows each use’ own notification settings. The app sends no email of its own.

4. Third parties

None. There are no external services, no sub-processors, no advertising and no analytics. The app makes no network calls outside the Atlassian platform.

5. Retention and deletion

Review dates live in your Atlassian tenancy for as long as the app is installed. Removing a schedule from a page deletes that pag’ record immediately. Uninstalling the app removes all data the app stored, in line with Atlassia’ Forge storage lifecycle. Because no personal data is stored, there is nothing about an individual to erase.

6. Your rights

Because all data sits inside your own Atlassian tenancy, your Confluence administrator controls it. For access, correction or deletion requests, or any privacy question, contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.
